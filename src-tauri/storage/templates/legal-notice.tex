% Legal Notice — Persistas & Partners house format.
%
% Modelled on the firm's own notices. The structure is fixed and the language is
% largely settled; what varies between one notice and the next is the parties,
% the facts, the sums and the number of numbered sections.
%
% The declared fields, their types and their validation live in
% legal-notice.json. Keep the two in step — a test fails if they drift.
%
% ON THE REPEATING PARTS
%
% A real notice carries N numbered sections, N payment tranches and N annexures.
% None of those counts is knowable when the template is written. Until the
% registry supports repeating groups they arrive as three assembled blocks that
% Keel builds — SECTIONS_BLOCK, ANNEXURES_BLOCK — using the macros defined in
% _shared/persist-letterhead.tex. See PROGRESS.md for the design.
