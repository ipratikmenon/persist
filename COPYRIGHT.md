## What this covers

Everything in this repository, including:

| Part | Description |
|---|---|
| Persist Desktop | The firm application — Keel (`src-tauri/`) and Deck (`src/`) |
| Persist Web | The client portal — `portal/backend/`, `portal/frontend/` |
| Sync server | `server/` |
| Document templates | `src-tauri/storage/templates/` |
| Specifications | `specs/`, and the product requirements document |

The document templates warrant particular mention: they encode the firm's own
precedents, drafting conventions and accumulated practice before the Indian
registries and courts. They are professional work product, not configuration.

## Confidentiality

This software is built to hold privileged and confidential client information
belonging to the clients of Persistas & Partners. Source code, database
contents, document vault contents and logs may all reveal the existence, subject
matter or status of a client engagement.

Anyone with access to this repository or to a running instance is bound by the
firm's professional obligations of confidentiality, whether or not they are an
advocate.
